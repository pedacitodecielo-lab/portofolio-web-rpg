04 About

Background and trajectory.

I am a final-year Meteorology student at Institut Teknologi Bandung with a working GPA of 4.00 / 4.00 in semester 7. My academic background sits at the intersection of atmospheric science, geospatial analysis, and computational modeling. Across three internships and several research projects, I have learned to treat data quality, validation, and reproducibility as non-negotiables.

I am pivoting from atmospheric research into data analytics and data engineering because the skill set transfers cleanly. Bias correction is feature engineering. Time-series analysis applies the same to revenue as to rainfall. ETL pipelines are ETL pipelines regardless of the source. My goal is to bring rigorous scientific habits to business problems.

Outside academic work, I lead the Research and Development Division at Atmosphaira, the ITB Meteorology Student Association, where I manage a 10-person team building IoT-based early warning instruments for a partner village.
